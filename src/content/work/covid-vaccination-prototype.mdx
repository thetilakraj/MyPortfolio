A mobile prototype for booking a COVID-19 vaccination appointment and verifying
its status, designed in early 2021 while the real booking experience was still
being figured out in public.

_Academic coursework produced during the postgraduate UX Design programme at
Humber College. A student concept — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built
for any health authority, and never intended to be mistaken for an official
service._

The interesting constraint was anxiety. People using a service like this are
worried, often on someone else's behalf, and frequently not the person the
interface was designed for. That pushed the design toward guest access without
an account, confirmation states that over-communicate rather than under-, and
plain language everywhere.

It is the first time I designed for a user in a bad mood rather than a
convenient one, which turned out to be most of what interface design actually
is.
