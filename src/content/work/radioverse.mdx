Radioverse treats the **station** as the primary object, not the track. The
promise is curated, music-only live radio discovery with no ads.

## Situation

_Phase 3 writes the full case study to the six-part spine: situation,
constraints, decisions, what shipped, outcome, and what I'd change._

## Constraints

Solo build. Live audio cannot be faked offline, billing has to be real, and a
catalogue assembled from third-party directories is hostile by default — dead
streams, duplicates, and mislabelled genres.

## Decisions

The design system locks to one direction: a restrained physical audio
instrument. Amber is signal-only — active station, playback state, premium
markers — and never decoration. That single rule does most of the visual work.
