A team evaluation of Netflix's discovery and viewing experience, produced during
the postgraduate UX Design programme at Humber College. The report presents the
findings and the design responses we proposed.

_Academic coursework. Not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by
Netflix._

It is kept here because it is where I learned to separate an observation from an
opinion — to write "seven of nine participants scrolled past the row without
stopping" instead of "the row doesn't work." That distinction is the entire
difference between research and preference, and it is the habit I use now when
reviewing vendor work.
