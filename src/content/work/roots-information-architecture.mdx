An information architecture study of a Canadian retail site — mapping how the
content was organised, observing how people actually moved through it, and
identifying where the two diverged.

_Academic coursework produced during the postgraduate UX Design programme at
Humber College. Not affiliated with or commissioned by the retailer._

The lesson that stuck: a navigation structure usually mirrors how the
organisation is arranged internally, not how customers think about what they
want. Recognising that shape is now the first thing I look for when reviewing
an enterprise launchpad, where the same failure is close to universal.
